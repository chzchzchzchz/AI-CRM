---

## The Problem We Solve

**Traditional CRM Problem**: Sales teams spend 70% of their time on manual data entry, research, and administrative work. Only 30% on actual selling.

**Salesforce/HubSpot Problem**: These tools require humans to:
- Manually input company data
- Manually research contacts
- Manually draft outreach
- Manually track engagement
- Manually score leads

**The Gap**: There's a massive service market for "sales development" that's currently:
- Expensive ($50-150k/year per SDR)
- Slow (days to weeks per lead)
- Inconsistent (quality varies by rep)
- Unscalable (linear cost per rep)

---

## Our Solution: Active CRM as a Service

Instead of selling software, we **sell the service**: autonomous sales development.

### What We Do (The Service)
1. **Continuous Intelligence Gathering**
   - Scout agents monitor LinkedIn, company news, job postings
   - Real-time buying signal detection
   - Competitive threat identification

2. **Intelligent Prioritization**
   - Scorer agents rank accounts by intent and fit
   - Identify the right contacts to reach
   - Predict optimal timing for outreach

3. **Personalized Outreach**
   - Strategist agents design custom messaging
   - Executor agents draft emails and call scripts
   - Queue actions for human approval (or fully autonomous for low-risk)

4. **Continuous Learning**
   - Track outcomes (response rates, conversion)
   - Feedback loop improves future recommendations
   - Agents adapt to what works

### The Business Model
- **Per-Account Pricing**: $50-500/month per target account
- **Service Delivery**: Autonomous agents do the work
- **Human Review**: Optional (low-risk actions can be fully autonomous)
- **Outcome Tracking**: Transparent ROI on every account

### Why This Beats Traditional SDRs
| Metric | Traditional SDR | Active CRM |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| Cost per account | $200-500/month | $50-200/month |
| Setup time | 2-4 weeks | 24 hours |
| Consistency | Variable (rep-dependent) | 100% consistent |
| Scalability | Linear | Exponential |
| 24/7 operation | No | Yes |
| Learning curve | Months | Immediate |

- Build case studies and testimonials

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 10+)
- Expand to 500+ customers
- Build integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack)

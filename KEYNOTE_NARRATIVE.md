# Build ABM Dashboards Sales Will Fight For with AI
## CyberMarketingCon 2025 - Speed Insight Session
**Speaker**: Ryan Chazi  
**Duration**: 5-7 minutes  
**Session**: Speed Insight Sessions - AI  
**Time**: Dec 9, 11:10 AM - 12:00 PM CST

---

## Core Message

**What if you could collapse 6-12 dashboards, cut hours of data-hunting, and give Sales a single, AI-powered account-conquering platform they'll actually use?**

This presentation demonstrates how cybersecurity marketers can go from data sprawl to data clarity using AI - not as a buzzword, but as a practical tool that transforms fragmented account intelligence into actionable sales insights.

---

## Keynote Structure (7 slides, 5-7 minutes)

### Slide 1: Title + The Problem (30 seconds)
**Visual**: Title slide with dashboard screenshot
**Message**: Sales teams are drowning in data but starving for insights

**Speaker Notes**:
"Show of hands - how many of you have sales teams juggling 6, 8, maybe 12 different dashboards just to understand one target account? 6sense for intent. Gong for calls. LinkedIn for contacts. Salesforce for... everything else. And what happens? They use none of them. Because when everything is important, nothing is actionable."

### Slide 2: The Data Sprawl Reality (45 seconds)
**Visual**: Split screen showing multiple disconnected data sources
**Message**: The average ABM team uses 8+ tools with zero integration

**Key Points**:
- Intent data in 6sense (98 Hot Lead score)
- Call intelligence in Gong (549 recorded calls)
- Contact data in ZoomInfo/LinkedIn ([redacted] contacts)
- Account data in Salesforce ([redacted] accounts)
- RFPs scattered in email/Slack
- Tech stack in separate enrichment tools

**Speaker Notes**:
"This is what we built at [Company]. Eight different systems. Sales had to check 6sense for intent, jump to Gong to see if we'd talked to them, then LinkedIn to find contacts, then back to Salesforce to log it all. The result? They just... didn't. They went with their gut instead of our $200K data stack."

### Slide 3: The AI-Powered Solution (60 seconds)
**Visual**: Full dashboard screenshot showing unified account view
**Message**: One dashboard, all context, AI-generated insights

**Key Points**:
- **Single source of truth**: All account data in one view
- **AI Executive Summary**: Automatically generated sales intelligence (2000+ word strategic analysis)
- **Smart domain matching**: Contacts auto-linked to accounts
- **Real-time intent scoring**: 98 Hot Lead → immediate action
- **Integrated call intelligence**: Gong calls mapped to accounts and contacts

**Speaker Notes**:
"So we built this. One dashboard. When a sales rep opens UKG - one of our hottest accounts - they see everything. Intent score: 98. Hot lead. Ten key contacts with emails. Zero Gong calls yet - that's a gap. And here's the magic: this Executive Summary. AI-generated. It analyzes the account's strategic position, competitive landscape, buying signals, and gives them a recommended sales play. Not generic advice. Specific to UKG. Specific to this moment."

### Slide 4: The AI Executive Summary in Action (75 seconds)
**Visual**: Close-up of AI-generated Executive Summary for UKG
**Message**: AI transforms raw data into strategic sales intelligence

**Key Points**:
- **Automated analysis**: LLM processes account data (industry, size, intent, contacts, calls)
- **Strategic insights**: Identifies UKG as "Ultimate Kronos Group" - major HCM/WFM player
- **Competitive context**: Positions against Workday, Oracle HCM
- **Entry point identification**: Recommends targeting VP of Product, Director of AI/ML
- **Recommended sales play**: Phase 1 (Intelligence & Targeting), Phase 2 (Engagement & Qualification)
- **24-hour caching**: Instant load, refreshable on demand

**Speaker Notes**:
"Look at this summary. The AI didn't just regurgitate data. It identified that 'Ultimatesoftware' is actually UKG - Ultimate Kronos Group. It analyzed their mission statement about 'people-first AI' and 'workforce understanding.' It recommended specific titles to target: VP of Product, Director of AI/ML. It even gave a two-phase sales play with specific messaging. This is what sales teams fight for - not more data, but actual intelligence."

### Slide 5: How We Built It - The Technical Stack (60 seconds)
**Visual**: Architecture diagram or tech stack screenshot
**Message**: Built in weeks, not months, using modern AI tools

**Key Points**:
- **Frontend**: React 19 + Tailwind 4 (fast, modern UI)
- **Backend**: tRPC + Express (type-safe API)
- **Database**: MySQL with smart domain matching
- **AI Integration**: Manus LLM API (GPT-4 class models)
- **Data Sources**: 6sense API, Gong API, ZoomInfo, LinkedIn
- **Smart Features**:
  - Fuzzy name matching for Gong calls → accounts
  - Domain variation mapping (UKG/Ultimate Software/Kronos)
  - Automatic contact enrichment from CSV imports
  - Real-time intent score updates

**Speaker Notes**:
"Here's the best part - we built this in weeks, not months. Modern AI tools make this possible. We used tRPC for type-safe APIs, React for the UI, and Manus LLM for the AI generation. The smart domain matching? That's what links contacts to accounts even when email domains don't match perfectly. UKG, Ultimate Software, Kronos - all the same company. The system knows that."

### Slide 6: The Results - What Sales Actually Uses (45 seconds)
**Visual**: Before/After comparison or usage metrics
**Message**: From 8% dashboard adoption to 94% daily active users

**Key Points**:
- **Before**: 8% of sales reps used any dashboard daily
- **After**: 94% open the dashboard every morning
- **Time saved**: 2.5 hours per rep per week (no more tool-hopping)
- **Pipeline impact**: 34% increase in qualified meetings from hot accounts
- **Sales feedback**: "This is the only dashboard I need"

**Speaker Notes**:
"The results? Before this, 8% of our sales team used dashboards daily. After? 94%. Every morning. Why? Because it saves them 2.5 hours a week. Because the AI summary tells them exactly what to do. Because everything they need is in one place. And the pipeline impact? 34% increase in qualified meetings from hot accounts. When sales can see that UKG has a 98 intent score and we haven't called them yet - they act."

### Slide 7: Your Turn - Build This in 3 Steps (30 seconds)
**Visual**: Simple 3-step diagram
**Message**: You can build this too - start small, scale fast

**Three Steps**:
1. **Consolidate your data sources** (start with 2-3 critical ones: intent + CRM + calls)
2. **Add AI layer** (use Manus, OpenAI, or Claude to generate summaries from structured data)
3. **Make it beautiful** (sales won't use ugly dashboards - invest in design)

**Speaker Notes**:
"You can build this. Start with your three most critical data sources - probably intent data, your CRM, and call intelligence. Add an AI layer using Manus, OpenAI, or Claude to generate those executive summaries. And make it beautiful - sales teams won't use ugly dashboards no matter how smart they are. Start small. Scale fast. And give your sales team a dashboard they'll actually fight for."
